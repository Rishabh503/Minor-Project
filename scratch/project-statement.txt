1. TITLE OF THE PROJECT

Role-Based Smart Employee Onboarding and Work Simulation Platform.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Employee onboarding processes vary significantly across organizations and teams and are often dependent
on informal communication, scattered documents, meetings, and manual task allocation. New employees
may spend their initial days going through policies and training material without getting sufficient exposure
to the type of work they will actually perform in their roles.

Another challenge is that new employees may hesitate to ask questions during the initial onboarding period
due to the fear of asking something they consider basic or insignificant. As a result, managers may not have a
clear understanding of the common areas of confusion among new employees.

The proposed project aims to address these issues by providing a structured, role-based onboarding platform
where companies can define onboarding roadmaps, provide role-specific historical work tasks, organize
meetings and courses, and monitor trainee progress. The platform will also provide an anonymous doubt
forum and an AI-based assistant using company-provided policies, FAQs, and resources to support trainees
throughout the onboarding process.

3. WHY IS THE PARTICULAR TOPIC CHOSEN?

The topic was chosen because employee onboarding is an important process that directly affects how quickly
new employees understand their responsibilities, organizational practices, and team workflows. However,
onboarding is often handled differently across companies and even across different teams within the same
organization.

A major gap identified is that onboarding generally focuses on sharing information rather than allowing
trainees to practice the type of work they will perform. Providing sanitized historical tasks and role-specific
work simulations can allow trainees to gain practical exposure in a safe environment before working on
actual company tasks.

The project also focuses on the communication gap between trainees and managers. An anonymous doubt
forum can encourage trainees to raise questions more comfortably, while AI-based analysis can identify
common areas of confusion and present summarized insights to managers. A company-specific AI assistant
can further provide immediate answers based on available organizational policies, FAQs, and onboarding
resources.

Therefore, the topic provides an opportunity to combine structured onboarding, practical work simulation,
progress monitoring, and AI-assisted support into a single platform.
4. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE OF THE PROJECT

4.1. Objectives

    1.​ To develop a centralized platform for organizing and standardizing the employee onboarding
         process.

    2.​ To allow companies to define role-specific onboarding plans containing meetings, courses, policies,
         and practical tasks.

    3.​ To provide role-based historical work simulations that allow trainees to practice realistic tasks before
         working in the actual company environment.

    4.​ To provide managers with tools to create onboarding schedules and monitor the progress and
         performance of trainees.

    5.​ To develop an anonymous doubt forum where trainees can raise questions and interact with other
         trainees without revealing their identities.

    6.​ To analyze and categorize trainee questions using AI to identify common areas of confusion and
         provide summarized insights to managers.

    7.​ To provide an AI-based onboarding assistant that can answer trainee queries using
         company-provided policies, FAQs, documentation, and historical problem information.

    8.​ To provide progress and performance insights that help managers identify areas where trainees may
         require additional guidance.

4.2. Scope

The proposed system will initially demonstrate the onboarding process for three different role categories:
Human Resources, Software Development, and Issue Handling/Analytics. The system will provide a
common onboarding framework while allowing role-specific tasks, resources, and learning requirements.

The company or administrator will be able to upload policies and security documents, define roles, add
historical tasks, and configure onboarding roadmaps based on the duration and requirements of the program.
Managers will be able to create and modify schedules containing meetings, courses, and tasks, while trainees
will access these activities through a personalized onboarding dashboard.

The system will include a role-based task simulation module, anonymous doubt discussion, AI-based
categorization of common trainee concerns, and a company-specific AI assistant. The initial implementation
will focus on a prototype environment and will not include direct integration with production HR systems or
automatic provisioning of access to real company tools.
5. METHODOLOGY

The project will follow a modular software development approach in which the onboarding process is
divided into company management, role-based task management, scheduling, trainee activities, and
AI-assisted support.

5.1 Proposed Methodology

    1.​ Requirement Analysis​
           Identify the onboarding requirements of companies, managers, and trainees and define common
         features applicable to different roles.

    2.​ Role and Content Configuration​
           Companies will define roles, upload policies/FAQs, and add role-specific historical work tasks,
         courses, and other onboarding resources.

    3.​ Onboarding Roadmap Creation​
           The system will generate a structured onboarding roadmap based on the selected duration and
         activities. Managers can customize the schedule of meetings, courses, and tasks.

    4.​ Trainee Onboarding​
           Trainees will access a personalized dashboard and calendar containing their assigned meetings,
         courses, documents, and role-based work simulations.

    5.​ Work Simulation and Evaluation​
          Historical work scenarios will be presented as simulated tasks. Trainees will submit their solutions,
         which will be evaluated using predefined criteria and, where appropriate, AI-assisted analysis.

    6.​ Anonymous Doubt Management​
           Trainees can post questions anonymously and interact with other trainees through reactions and
         discussions. AI will categorize recurring questions and generate summarized insights for managers.

    7.​ AI Onboarding Assistant​
          A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system will use company-provided policies, FAQs, and
         onboarding resources to answer trainee queries and provide contextual guidance.

    8.​ Progress and Manager Insights​
          The system will track completion and task performance and provide managers with trainee progress,
         common areas of difficulty, and onboarding insights.

    9.​ Testing and Validation​
            The system will be tested using the selected HR, Software Development, and Issue

         Handling/Analytics roles to validate functionality, usability, and the accuracy of AI-assisted features.

7. WHAT CONTRIBUTION WOULD THE PROJECT MAKE TOWARDS
THE SOCIETY

The project aims to improve the transition of individuals into new workplaces by providing a structured and
accessible onboarding environment. It enables employees to understand their roles through practical work
simulations, learning resources, and continuous guidance. The anonymous doubt forum encourages trainees
to ask questions without hesitation, while AI-based analysis helps organizations identify common areas of
confusion and improve their onboarding process. The system can therefore support skill development,
workplace preparedness, inclusive communication, and more efficient organizational practices.

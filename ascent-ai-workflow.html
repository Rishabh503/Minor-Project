Ascent AI workflows

Company material flows into a private knowledge base; employees and managers use it through role-scoped AI tools.

Manager adds company material

  1. 01Sign in as a company admin or manager.
  2. ↓
  3. 02Open Knowledge Base.
  4. ↓
  5. 03Enter a title and paste policy, FAQ, or onboarding text.
  6. ↓
  7. 04Server checks role, company, title, and text length.
  8. ↓
  9. 05Text is split into chunks; Gemini creates embeddings.
  10. ↓
  11. 06Chunks and embeddings are stored under that company in PostgreSQL.

Text paste is supported; PDF and Word upload are not implemented.

Employee asks a question

  1. 01Sign in and open AI Onboarding Help.
  2. ↓
  3. 02Ask a question about company onboarding.
  4. ↓
  5. 03Gemini embeds the question; the server searches only this company’s chunks.
  6. ↓
  7. 04Cosine similarity selects the four closest chunks.
  8. ↓
  9. 05Gemini drafts an answer from retrieved material and returns source titles.
  10. ↓
  11. 06If material does not answer it, the assistant directs the employee to their manager.

Written-answer grading suggestions

Assessment submission → suggest grades → manager reviews and edits → manager saves or releases. AI suggestions are never released automatically.

Forum doubt insights

Analytics → analyze questions → Gemini groups recurring themes. Managers see direct-report questions; company admins see company-scoped questions.

Architecture

Browser UI→ Next.js auth, role checks, server actions→ Prisma / PostgreSQL+ Gemini embeddings and generation
